# Final Destination

The **Final Destination** franchise is one of horror cinema’s most creative and enduring sagas. Created by Jeffrey Reddick, the series turns the concept of a slasher movie on its head: instead of a masked killer, the antagonist is **Death itself**—an invisible, unstoppable force enforcing its divine master plan.
